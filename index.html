Two ways to plan

Pick the one that suits you.

Every Kofvi planner comes in one of two formats. Both are instant digital downloads, and both work on your laptop, tablet and phone.

Google Sheets templates

Make your own copy with one click and keep it in your Google Drive forever.

  • Works with a free Google account
  • Totals and charts update by themselves
  • Change the currency and names in one place

Web app planners

Open your planner in any browser. Sign in with your email and it syncs to every device.

  • Nothing to install

  • Your plan stays private to your account

About Kofvi

Planning should feel like a slow morning.

Kofvi started with a simple idea: planners should be easy to use and lovely to look at. Too many templates are packed with tabs you never open and formulas you are scared to touch.

So every Kofvi planner keeps things simple. One place for your details, clear pages, and a design that makes you want to open it again tomorrow, ideally with a warm cup in hand.

FAQ

Good questions.

Where can I buy a planner?

All Kofvi planners are sold in our Etsy shop. Etsy handles the secure checkout, and your download is ready right after you pay.

Do I need Excel or any special software?

No. Google Sheets templates work with a free Google account, and web app planners open in any browser.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Both formats work on phones and tablets. Planning is most comfortable on a laptop, but checking and ticking things off on your phone works well.

Is it a physical product?

No. Every Kofvi planner is a digital download. Nothing is shipped.

I already bought a web app planner. Where do I sign in?

Go to kofvi.com/planner and sign in with the email you used before. New buyers will find their access code in the PDF that came with the purchase.
